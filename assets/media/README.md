# Media

The clips are short, silent excerpts (H.264, 960×540) with native controls.

- `bloom-clearing.mp4`, `bloom-water.mp4`, `bloom-creature.mp4`: silent loops from my film *Bloom* (1:48–1:54, 1:02–1:10, and about 2:12). Artwork, animation and compositing are mine; the soundtrack is left out. For the loops, the clearing and water shots have their camera move held still (the crop follows the scenery) and a short cross-fade where the end meets the start. The creature loop is a 960×540 crop of the full-resolution frame, cut where the drawings match, with only the fire cross-faded at the join.
- `hotpot-process.mp4`: 0:02–0:20 of my timelapse for the hotpot illustration (ZZZ 2025 Art Fest). The four process stills come from the same recording.
- `dungeon-heatmap.mp4` and `dungeon-flow.mp4`: recordings from my Godot prototype, made with Godot's Movie Maker. The heatmap shows position scores and the movement target in automatic combat; the flow clip shows the exploration flow field, which is only part of how units move. Sprites and environment art are third-party.

Other images:

- Whig–Clio: the image I was given and my restored seals (the seated figure and the bird). These are existing university marks that I restored, not new designs.
- Scully: my crane artwork and a photo of the finished sweatshirt. The MOL and reunion images show the artwork, not the printed garments.
- Class of 2023 jacket: photo by Denise Applewhite, Princeton University Office of Communications ([source](https://www.princeton.edu/news/2023/05/29/us-rep-terri-sewell-86-princeton-seniors-class-day-spread-seeds-service-wherever)).
