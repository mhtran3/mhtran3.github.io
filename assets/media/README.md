# Selected media

All excerpts are silent H.264 at 960x540, with faststart and native controls. They are compressed copies of the original recordings; no footage is generated or composited into the game.

- **bloom-clearing.mp4**: Michael Tran's Bloom final film, 1:48–1:54. Visual artwork, animation, and compositing by Michael. Soundtrack omitted.
- **hotpot-process.mp4**: 0:02–0:20 of Michael's IMG_9985 timelapse. Four stills show the source at 0:10, 0:17.5, 0:40, and 0:55. Layer visibility changes are part of the original process recording. Festival context supplied by Michael: ZZZ 2025 Art Fest.
- **dungeon-heatmap.mp4**: 1:44–1:57 of the October 14, 2026 Godot viewport capture, Enchanted Grove. Existing automatic combat, with position scores and movement target visible.
- **dungeon-flow.mp4**: 0:58–1:12 of the same capture. Existing exploration flow field is displayed during combat; it is not the whole combat steering policy.

Game recordings used isolated save data and the existing Godot Movie Maker. No game code or outcomes were altered for the recording. Sprites and environment art are third-party assets; the case study describes system design, AI-assisted development, and testing. These excerpts are demonstrations, not benchmarks or proof of globally optimal behavior.


## Applied design images

- Whig–Clio before/after: provided paired source image (Whig and Clio Seals.png), and Michael’s Clio2.png (seated figure) and Whig2.png (bird) restoration exports, resized and losslessly encoded for the website. The house/building seal is excluded at Michael’s request. Research and restoration for official university use, including recovery of features lost to compression, are confirmed by Michael. These are existing institutional marks, not new identities invented for this portfolio.
- Scully: original crane artwork and FullSizeRender_213.jpg from Michael’s portfolio Drive. Photograph shows the finished sweatshirt. Michael confirmed Scully, MOL, and Class of 1976 designs were printed. MOL and reunion website images are artwork previews; finished-garment photos for those two have not been selected.
- Class of 2023 jacket: photo by Denise Applewhite, Princeton University Office of Communications, from https://www.princeton.edu/news/2023/05/29/us-rep-terri-sewell-86-princeton-seniors-class-day-spread-seeds-service-wherever . The photo shows jackets on chairs; no generated or reconstructed garment image is used. The page directly credits Michael as designer. Additional design coverage: https://paw.princeton.edu/article/punk-panels-and-perfect-weather-mark-reunions-2023 . The portrait from that article is not used on the site.
